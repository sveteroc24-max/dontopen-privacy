Privacy Policy - Horror Game

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Don't Open: Horror Game is a mobile horror game developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

1. Information and Game Progress

The game is designed to work offline and does not require account registration. Game progress, decisions, and settings may be stored locally on your device.

The developer does not operate a game server for collecting player accounts or game progress.

2. Advertising

The game may display advertisements using Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information including IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and ad interactions.

Such processing may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable laws and consent requirements.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Storage

Game progress and preferences may be saved locally on the device. The developer does not intentionally transmit this local game progress to its own servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The game may use Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The game contains horror themes and is not specifically designed for children. The intended audience and age classification will follow the game's Google Play declarations.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can remove locally stored game progress by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to your device's backup settings.

Advertising privacy choices, where applicable, are provided in accordance with relevant platform requirements and consent mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
